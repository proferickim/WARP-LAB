Welcome to WARP Lab

We study wireless AI and radio perception, connecting intelligent algorithms with practical wireless systems.

Led by Prof. Yonghwi Kim, our research brings together signal processing, optimization, and machine learning for next-generation communications and sensing. We develop and evaluate ideas through over-the-air experiments, software-defined radios, and system-level simulations.
